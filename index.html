📊 10X STOCK ZEROの分析について
10X STOCK ZEROは、Japan Stocks・U.S. StocksのMarketデータから多数の銘柄をスクリーニングし、 流動性を考慮したうえで、複数の指標を組み合わせて総合評価しています。

主な5つの分析項目
・企業の質・成長性 25%
・Technical分析 25%
・Valuation 20%
・Financials安全性 15%
・成長材料 15%

Technical分析では、20日・50日・200日移動平均、RSI、MACD、 Volume、株価モメンタムなどを使用しています。 Financials・企業分析では、PER、PBR、PSR、売上成長、利益成長、 ROE、利益率、キャッシュフロー、負債水準などを参考にしています。

Short Term・Mid Term・Long Termではそれぞれ異なる条件を適用し、 各時間軸に適した銘柄を評価してTOP10を選定しています。

※ ScoreやRankingは将来の株価上昇や利益を保証するものではありません。 Market環境や企業情報の変化により評価は変動します。 最終的な投資判断は利用者ご自身で行ってください。

User Guide

1. Select Market and Time Horizon
Choose Japan Stocks or U.S. Stocks, then Short Term (up to 6 months), Mid Term (6–12 months), or Long Term (1+ year). The TOP 10 ranking updates to match your selection.
2. TOP 10 Ranking
View ticker/code, company name, score, and grade. Red indicates upside potential, Blue steady growth, and Yellow growth stocks. These are analytical characteristics, not direct trading instructions.
3. Update Times
U.S. stock ranking data is generally updated around 06:30 JST and Japanese stock data around 16:00 JST. Market holidays or data availability may delay updates.
4. Buy Timing Analysis
Enter a 4-digit Japanese stock code or a U.S. ticker and tap Analyze. Results include current price, overall rating, technical/trend gauges, reasons, and factor scores. Ratings are Strong Buy, Buy, Caution, and Wait.
5. Detailed Stock Analysis
Select the market and enter the ticker/code. When available, company name, current price, and analysis values are filled automatically. The five factors total 100 points: Valuation 20, Quality & Growth 25, Financials 15, Technical 25, and Catalysts 15.
6. Save Results
Save results to Analysis History, Portfolio, or Favorites for later review.
7. Market Links
Use Top Gainers, Top Losers, Volume, Unusual Volume, Earnings Calendar, 52-Week Highs, Sector Performance, and Screener links to explore Japanese and U.S. markets in English.
8. Portfolio and Favorites
Track saved stocks, shares, purchase price, current price, and profit/loss information.
9. Settings and Backup
Export/import saved data, clear history, review legal information, and access support.
Important Notice
Scores, rankings, and Buy Timing Analysis are analytical reference tools. They do not recommend buying or selling any security and do not guarantee future gains. Review current information and make final investment decisions at your own discretion.
