Privacy Policy

Effective: August 23, 2026

10X STOCK ZERO (the “App”) is a tool designed to assist users in viewing and analyzing stock information. This policy explains how user information is handled in the App.

1. Information Handled

The App may handle information entered or saved by users, including ticker symbols, holdings, favorites, and analysis history. Before release of the commercial version, the actual storage locations, whether information is transmitted externally, and the third-party services used will be finally reviewed and accurately reflected in this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures.

2. Purpose of Use

Information entered by users is used to provide stock analysis, holdings management, favorites, analysis history, and other App functions.

3. Third-Party Services and External Communications

The App may send requests to external information providers or communication services to obtain stock prices, company information, and related data. For the official commercial version, this section will be updated after the services used and information transmitted have been finalized.

4. Storage and Deletion

Information stored on the device is designed to be removable through App functions or by deleting the App. If server-side storage is added in the future, the storage period and deletion method will be stated.

5. Advertising and Tracking

The current pre-release commercial version does not assume tracking for advertising purposes. If such functionality is added in the future, required consent procedures and privacy disclosures will be updated.

6. Changes

This policy may be revised in response to changes in App functions, external services, laws, or regulations.
